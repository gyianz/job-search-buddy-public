Add jobs from LinkedIn and SEEK

Drag this button to your browser's bookmarks bar:

+ Add to Job Board
  1. Open a job on LinkedIn or SEEK (or any careers page) as you normally would.
  2. Click + Add to Job Board in your bookmarks bar.
  3. A new tab opens on your board with the title, company, location, link and description filled in. Check them and click Add job.

If the description doesn't come through, select the description text on the page first, then click the bookmark: anything you've selected is used as the description.

The bookmark only reads the page you're looking at, in your own browser, like copying and pasting it. It doesn't load any other pages or use your account for anything else, and the job details go straight to this board at {{ origin }}. If you start the board on a different address or port, come back here and drag the button again.
