Scoring rubric

The instructions Claude follows when it judges each posting · saved in {{ path }}
How judging works. When you ask Claude to process the scoring queue, it runs app.cli rubric to read these rules, reads your profile and the posting, then records a bucket, a 1–5 score per criterion and a rationale with app.cli score. Changes here apply to the next job scored; already-scored jobs keep their scores until re-scored.
